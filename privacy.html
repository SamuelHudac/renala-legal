Renala — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Samuel Hudac ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Renala mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using Renala, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use Renala, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Name — used only for personalized greetings within the App
  • Doctor/Dietitian name — displayed on PDF reports you choose to share
  • CKD stage — used to set appropriate dietary limits

1.2 Health and Nutrition Data

Renala collects and stores the following health-related data locally on your device only:

  • Meal entries with estimated nutrient values (potassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein, fluids)
  • Laboratory test results (GFR, creatinine, BUN, potassium, phosphorus, sodium, hemoglobin, albumin)
  • Medication reminders (medication names, dosages, and schedules)
  • Favourite foods with nutritional information
  • Daily nutrient intake summaries and streaks
  • Personal dietary limits

This data is stored exclusively on your device using local database technology (Hive). We do not upload, transmit, or store this data on any external server. We have no access to your health data.

1.3 Camera and Image Data

When you use the AI meal scanning feature, Renala:

  • Captures a photo of your food using your device's camera
  • Transmits the image to Google's Gemini API over an encrypted HTTPS connection for nutritional analysis
  • Receives estimated nutritional values in return
  • Does not store the image after analysis is complete

Image processing is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Generative AI Terms of Service.

1.4 Barcode Scanning

When you scan a product barcode, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts API, an open-source public database. No personal information is transmitted with the barcode query.

1.5 Advertising Data (Free Users Only)

For users on the free plan, Renala displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address (for ad targeting and fraud prevention)
  • General location (country/region level, not precise GPS)

Premium subscribers do not see any advertisements and no advertising-related data is collected for premium users. For more information, see Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

1.6 Subscription Data

Renala uses RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions. RevenueCat processes:

  • Purchase receipts from Google Play
  • Subscription status (active, expired, or in trial)
  • Anonymous user identifiers

For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

1.7 Push Notifications

If you enable meal or medication reminders, Renala schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is transmitted to any server. Notification scheduling is handled entirely on-device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide the core functionality of the App (nutrition tracking, lab tracking, medication reminders)
  • Generate PDF reports that you choose to share with your healthcare provider
  • Display relevant advertisements to free-plan users
  • Process premium subscription purchases
  • Send local reminder notifications that you have opted into

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your health data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

All health data (meals, labs, medications, favourites, settings) is stored locally on your device using Hive, a lightweight local database. This data:

  • Never leaves your device (except food images temporarily sent for AI analysis)
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service by us
  • Is protected by your device's own security measures (lock screen, encryption)
  • Is removed when you uninstall the App

Data transmitted to third-party services (Google Gemini API, Open Food Facts, RevenueCat, Google AdMob) is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your health data is retained on your device until you choose to delete it. You can delete all your data at any time through:

Profile → Delete all my data

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion, no health data remains on your device. Uninstalling the Renala app also removes all locally stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

Renala uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API — AI food image analysis
  • Open Food Facts — barcode product lookup (open-source)
  • Google AdMob — advertising for free users
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchases

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

6. Your Rights (GDPR — EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — all your data is stored locally on your device and is directly accessible to you
  • Right to rectification — you can edit your profile, meals, and lab values at any time within the App
  • Right to erasure — you can delete all your data via Profile → Delete all my data
  • Right to restrict processing — you can disable AI scanning, barcode scanning, and notifications at any time
  • Right to data portability — you can export your lab data as a PDF report
  • Right to object to advertising — you can choose non-personalized ads via the GDPR consent dialog, or upgrade to Premium to remove all ads

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Renala is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Medical Disclaimer

Renala is a dietary tracking tool and is NOT a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

  • AI-generated nutritional estimates are approximate and should not be used as the sole basis for dietary decisions
  • Laboratory reference ranges displayed in the App are informational only
  • All dietary limits should be set by your nephrologist or registered dietitian
  • Always consult your healthcare provider before making changes to your diet or medication regimen

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
