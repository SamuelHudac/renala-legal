2. Description of Service

Renala is a dietary tracking application designed to help individuals with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) monitor their nutritional intake, laboratory values, and medications. The App provides:

  • AI-powered meal scanning and nutritional estimation
  • Barcode scanning for packaged food products
  • Manual meal and nutrient logging
  • Laboratory value tracking with trend visualization
  • Medication reminders
  • PDF report generation for healthcare providers
  • Daily and weekly nutrient trend analysis
